Chat model quantization and KV-cache precision — 2026-10-13, RX 5600M (6 GiB, Vulkan), Ollama 0.33.3.
Two test servers beside the live one, same binary and models dir: f16 KV (stock) and
OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0 + OLLAMA_FLASH_ATTENTION=1. One job at a time, nothing else on the GPU,
card cooled to ≤65 °C before each run (it reaches 86–96 °C during one; fans at max).

   The 8-bit KV cache is a 4.5x prefill loss for qwen3:4b at Q4 (both Q4 files), and neutral or a
   gain for every other model here — including qwen3.5:4b, also Q4_K_M. Not understood; measured
   twice. So the setting is safe server-wide only once the chat model isn't the Q4 qwen3:4b.
   For scale, the live server (same Q4_K_M, f16 KV) read a 9311-token app prompt at 520–614 tok/s
   (journal, 07:24) — longer prompts run faster per token here.

3. MMLU-Pro, 280 questions stratified over 14 subjects, non-thinking, answer = most likely option
   letter after "The answer is (" (every question gets an answer; ~1.2 s each at 4K context)
   model                 KV     accuracy   same answer as q8_0
   Q4_K_M                f16    0.454      0.786
   UD-Q4_K_XL            f16    0.436      0.843
   Q6_K                  f16    0.446      0.929
   Q6_K                  q8_0   0.446      0.921     (267/280 answers identical to Q6_K f16)
   q8_0 (reference)      f16    0.468      1.000
   Accuracy differences are inside the noise (±3 points at n=280); agreement isn't: at Q4_K_M one
   answer in five differs from the 8-bit model's, at Q6_K one in thirteen.
   An earlier run that let the model write its answer lost 103/280 replies to explanations
   instead of a letter; that scoring was dropped.

4. The app's own tool choice (src/test/localToolEval.test.ts, LIVE_EVAL_EXTENDED=1: the 14 core
   requests + 25 long-tail ones; real roster with embeddinggemma, real system prompt and adapter)
   Q4_K_M, f16 KV:  27/39 (core 12/14), median 33 s/request
   Q6_K,  q8_0 KV:  25/37 before the old 30-min test limit cut the last two (core 11/14), median 32 s
   On the 37 both ran: 26 vs 25. They split on three requests (two to Q4_K_M, one to Q6_K) — noise.
   Most misses for both are a text reply where a tool call was wanted ("I'd be happy to help you
   restore…", "Could you please…") — a prompt/roster matter, not a quantization one.

Decision: chat = Unsloth Q6_K with OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0. Less VRAM than before (4.36 vs
4.66 GiB), ~5% slower prefill and decode than Q4_K_M at f16, answers much closer to the 8-bit
model (2–3), equal tool choice (4); Deep Research decodes 25% faster as a side effect. Applied by
setup-once.sh (needs sudo); local-up.sh warns while it's missing.
